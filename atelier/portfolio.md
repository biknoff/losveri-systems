# Atelier Verí — Portfolio

One-pagers for the pieces alongside CERCA (see [cerca/](cerca/) for the
full build). Labels are honest: what's built, what's only designed.

## Touch — BUILT

An earlier interactive-touch piece and the direct ancestor of CERCA's
sensing approach: capacitive/conductive sensing turned into live
audio-visual response. Evidence on the operator's EVO drive: a `Los Veri
Touch` project folder containing `Touch Project`, `Touch.bidule`,
`Touch2.bidule`, and the related `New Capacitance*.bidule` patches, plus
the `Beacon` / `BeaconII` / `CercaCapIIR` Arduino sketches that sit next to
CERCA's own firmware — the sensing lineage from Touch into CERCA is
visible directly in the file history, not just claimed.

## Plant-Based Music — BUILT

A piece that senses plants and turns that signal into generative music.
Evidence: a `PLANTS` folder inside the `COLORFUL SOUNDS` project on the
operator's EVO drive, plus a separate `Atelier Verí Plants` archive dated
2021, indicating the piece was built and iterated across more than one
period rather than a single one-off session.

## Grasping for Space — label pending (operator record: portfolio mostly built/exhibited; on-disk evidence not located this pass)

A piece concerned with gesture and spatial reach, distinct from
Chichigua below. Not located on the reachable drives during this pass;
documented in the operator's records. Labeled DESIGNED rather than BUILT
pending direct evidence.

## Colorful Sounds — BUILT

A synesthetic sound-and-color piece: the `COLORFUL SOUNDS` project folder
and a `Colorful Sounds.bidule` patch exist directly on the operator's EVO
drive (the same drive holding the `PLANTS` subfolder used in Plant-Based
Music above), confirming a real, exercised Bidule patch rather than a
concept only.

## Cromafonía — label pending (operator record: designed/built; on-disk evidence not located this pass)

A piece exploring color-to-sound mapping (the name itself names the
concept — "chroma" + "phonia"). Not located on the reachable drives during
this pass; documented in the operator's records. Labeled DESIGNED rather
than BUILT pending direct evidence.

## Chichigua — BUILT + FLOWN

A kite instrument — genuinely built and flown, not a simulation or a
tabletop model. Sensors and an IMU ride aloft on the kite itself, streaming
motion data down over OSC to drive music generation on the ground. This is
its own piece, distinct from Grasping for Space above, and the
operator-confirmed case of an instrument whose "performance space" is the
open air. Not located as files on the reachable drives during this pass;
documented in the operator's own, direct account of flying it.

## SoundMES — CONCEPT

A concept-stage idea for a sound-generation system, extending the Verí
instrument family beyond CERCA and Chichigua. Labeled CONCEPT plainly — no
build evidence exists for it on the reachable drives, and none is claimed.
