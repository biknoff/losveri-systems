## On what the architecture is actually for (not just the execution edge)

> "Architecture: most copiers run leader-to-follower. We built peer accounts executing the same
> intention in parallel. [...] That execution edge is the wedge, not the moat. Execution alone,
> someone can eventually copy — I'm not going to claim otherwise. What actually holds is the
> context layer: chart observations, the intention behind a trade, modifications, voice,
> collaboration — all of it living around the trade, so a user's history becomes a workspace
> instead of a pile of disconnected transactions. Add community distribution and switching cost
> on top of that, and *that's* what's hard to copy. Not the code."

This is the "together" half of the product stated directly: the chart-plus-voice-plus-sharing
layer isn't decoration on top of the trading engine, it's the stated differentiator — the
execution engine (`../engine/`) is treated as necessary infrastructure, not the product itself.

## On staying precise about what's proven versus what's a hypothesis

> "I don't know CAC yet, because I haven't run paid acquisition — I'm not going to invent a
> number to sound complete. [...] The next phase exists specifically to turn price, CAC, and
> retention from guesses into data."

> "I know what I know. I know what I don't. I built the next phase specifically to close that
> gap."

The document's own framing — precision over a looser claim, stated as the mentor's core note at
the top of the file — is the same stance this evidence directory tries to hold: label the running
surface as running, and the product vision as vision.

## On the distribution bet

> "My distribution bet is community, not paid acquisition. [...] I'm not going to make up a [...]
> number today — I'd rather run that experiment and measure conversion, activation, retention.
> Real numbers, not a guess."
