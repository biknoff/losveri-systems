# The theory behind SEER/PROBE — genuinely cross-disciplinary, honestly unbuilt

Spectral Minesweeper's built half (Cold-Firing, the V-chain) is a primitive, undirected search —
its own governing document calls it exactly that: **"blind search."** The theoretical apparatus it
is a first instance of draws on three different fields, and the blueprint is explicit that each is
a *candidate account*, held on the shelf and tested against the geometry rather than assumed:

**Physics — the Kepler epistemology.** Not the orbital mechanics; the *inversion of attention*.
Kepler did not begin by specifying the planet — he built a disciplined observational process
capable of revealing a faint, recurrent footprint (a transit signature) from which an unseen
object could be inferred. SEER is designed to search for the equivalent: not the generative
coordinate directly, but its footprint on the outcome distribution — structured residual
variance — inferring the coordinate from the footprint rather than assuming the coordinate is
already named. An earlier design pass demoted this to "just the tool that does an inverse-FFT
transform"; the current blueprint restores it explicitly to an *epistemology*, with the FFT as one
tool in service of it, not the substance of it.

**Biology — chemotaxis, held as a candidate, not a foundation.** Chemotaxis — how organisms
aggregate along a chemical gradient with no central coordinator — is one candidate account of how
the correspondence between terrain and manifestation-family might actually work: manifestations
"finding" fertile terrain the way a cell finds a gradient, without anything planning the search.
The apparatus's own governing rule forbids treating this as the mechanism (a lock explicitly bans
collapsing the whole search to one biological metaphor) while keeping it as a live candidate to
test the geometry against, alongside spectral decomposition and gradient-aggregation as other
shelved accounts. The discipline itself — name your candidate mechanisms, forbid committing to
any one prematurely — is the point, independent of which candidate (if any) turns out to fit.

**Statistics — likelihood-free inference, reshaped.** The apparatus is *shaped* like
simulation-based inference (estimating something about a process from simulator draws rather than
a closed-form likelihood) but its actual target is different from SBI-proper: not a Bayesian
posterior over named parameters, but discovery of the latent representation itself — which
coordinates even exist, before asking what their values are. Validated, if it is ever built, by
three explicit statistical gates: compression (many observed patterns collapse into one
coordinate), transfer (the coordinate holds out-of-sample, across market periods), and invariance
(different surface behaviors become the same thing once expressed in the coordinate) — with
compression treated as the easy, almost-free gate, and transfer/invariance held as the hard ones
where a spurious factor actually dies.

## Why this belongs in a hiring-facing repo

None of this is built. **SEER and PROBE are DESIGNED, not BUILT, and this repo says so plainly
everywhere they're mentioned** — the point of including the theory is not to claim more than what
exists, but to show that the unbuilt half has a real, cross-disciplinary intellectual foundation
rather than being an unscoped aspiration. The discipline that produced this framing is the same
self-correcting one documented in [LINEAGE.md](../../../../LINEAGE.md) § 2 — an earlier design pass
(v3) mistook the statistical *shadow* (SBI-proper) for the actual target, and the correction is
written down, not quietly fixed.
